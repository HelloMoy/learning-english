# The Vowel Sound /ɪ/ (e corta)

El sonido /ɪ/, como en "sit" o "bin", es un sonido vocálico corto y relajado. La lengua se eleva ligeramente y se posiciona hacia la parte frontal de la boca, pero no tan alto como en el sonido /iː/ (como en "see"). Vas a dominar este sonido porque es fundamental para una comunicación clara en inglés. Aparece en muchas palabras comunes como "fish," "lid" y "pick," y perfeccionarlo te ayudará a sonar más natural y seguro.

The /ɪ/ sound, as in "sit" or "bin," is a short, relaxed vowel sound. The tongue is slightly raised and positioned toward the front of the mouth, but not as high as in the /iː/ sound (as in "see"). You’re going to master this sound because it's essential for clear communication in English. It's found in many everyday words like "fish," "lid," and "pick," and getting it right will help you sound more natural and confident.
