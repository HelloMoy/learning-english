# Exercise 7 : Pronunciation Step-by-Step Lesson

## 🇪🇸 Español

### “Look” vs. “Luke” con _Modern Family_

Suenan parecido, pero no son lo mismo:

- **“Look”** es un verbo: mirar. En _Modern Family_ lo oyes cuando Phil quiere que todos vean algo: “Look at this!”
- **“Luke”** es un nombre propio, el equivalente a Lucas. Es el hijo de los Dunphy, famoso por lo torpe y gracioso que es.

**La diferencia clave:** uno es una acción, el otro es una persona. El contexto casi siempre te lo aclara — y cuando no, la vocal es lo que decide.

## 🇺🇸 English

### “Look” vs. “Luke” with _Modern Family_

They sound similar, but they're not the same thing:

- **“Look”** is a verb: to see or gaze at something. In _Modern Family_ you hear it when Phil wants everyone to look at something: “Look at this!”
- **“Luke”** is a proper noun, the equivalent of Lucas. He's the Dunphys' son, famous for being clumsy and funny.

**The key difference:** one is an action, the other is a person. Context almost always makes it clear — and when it doesn't, the vowel is what decides.
