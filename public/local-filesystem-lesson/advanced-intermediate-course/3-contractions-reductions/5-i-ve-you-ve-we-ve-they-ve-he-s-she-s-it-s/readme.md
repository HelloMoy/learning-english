# I've, You've, We've, They've, He's, She's, It's

## 🇪🇸 Español

### Contracciones con _have_ y _has_

_I've_, _you've_, _we've_, _they've_, _he's_, _she's_, _it's_: las contracciones del presente perfecto.

Son esenciales para entender el inglés americano hablado, porque en conversación real la forma completa prácticamente desaparece. Reconocerlas te ayuda a **escuchar mejor** antes incluso de que te ayude a hablar.

⚠️ Ojo con _he's_, _she's_ e _it's_: valen tanto para _is_ como para _has_. El contexto te dice cuál es.

## 🇺🇸 English

### Contractions with _have_ and _has_

_I've_, _you've_, _we've_, _they've_, _he's_, _she's_, _it's_: the present perfect contractions.

They're essential for understanding spoken American English, because in real conversation the full form all but disappears. Recognizing them helps you **listen better** before it even helps you speak.

⚠️ Watch out for _he's_, _she's_, and _it's_: they stand for both _is_ and _has_. Context tells you which.
